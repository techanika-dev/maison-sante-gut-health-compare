Does this sound familiar?

  • You’re bloated within an hour of most meals
  • Certain foods set you off and you can’t pin down which ones
  • Heartburn or reflux keeps coming back
  • You’re wiped out after eating, not just full
  • Some weeks your digestion is fine, other weeks it isn’t
  • Your bloodwork came back “normal” but you still don’t feel right

Why find the cause, not just manage the symptom

Bloating, fatigue after eating, and unpredictable digestion usually have a specific driver: bacterial imbalance, a food intolerance, low stomach acid, inflammation. Treat the symptom without finding the driver, and it comes back.
