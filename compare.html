Gut Health landing page – existing vs proposed

Maison Santé · prototype for discussion, 8 October 2026 · the live page was read only; nothing in production was changed

Existing – first screen (live page, screenshot 7 Oct)

  • Offer and doctor are visible straight away.
  • Button says “Talk to a Doctor”, but it scrolls to a three-question form and the chat is answered by reception.
  • Pay and Claim appears about halfway down a page that is roughly 16 phone screens long.
  • The headline is about bloating only, whichever ad was clicked.

Proposed – first screen (live prototype, click through it)

  • Same brand, same offer card, same approved wording where it exists.
  • Doctor named with her title; “tests are separate” and Pay and Claim stated before the first tap.
  • Button says what happens: “Check availability”.
  • Headline follows the ad (bloating, heartburn, irregular digestion).
